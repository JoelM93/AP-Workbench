Import Invoices (CSV)

Upload a CSV with this header row:
Vendor,Invoice Number,Invoice Date,Due Date,Amount,Status,Approval,Approver
Dates should be YYYY-MM-DD. Status can be Pending, Scheduled, or Paid. Vendors that don't exist yet are created automatically with Net 30 terms. Rows with a missing or invalid amount are skipped and reported below.

Tip: export first to get a correctly formatted file, then edit it and re-import.
